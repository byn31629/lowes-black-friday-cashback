## When does Lowe's Black Friday start?

Lowe's confirmed the dates in its official holiday announcement: Black Friday promotions begin **October 29**, 2026 and continue through **December 24**. Instead of one frantic weekend, the season is split into four phases, each with its own deals.

| Dates | What's happening |
| --- | --- |
| From Oct 29 | Black Friday deals begin: tools, appliances, holiday décor, outdoor power equipment, plus "Gifts Under" picks and free products with select tool purchases |
| Nov 16–27 | 12 Days of Deals (expanded from 10 days last year), new savings each day |
| Thanksgiving week | The biggest Black Friday savings of the season |
| Nov 26 (Thanksgiving) | Stores closed; online-only deals continue; members get early Doorbuster access |
| Nov 27 (Black Friday) | Doorbusters open to everyone |
| Around Nov 30 | Extended Cyber Deals through Cyber Monday |
| Dec 3–24 | December Deal Drops, new offers weekly until Christmas Eve |

Two things worth flagging from that table. First, Lowe's itself says the deepest cuts come in Thanksgiving week — the October 29 launch is real discounts, but it's the opening act. Second, Doorbusters (the limited-quantity, headline deals on things like appliances and power tools) don't fully open until November 27. Buying a fridge in mid-October because it looks discounted is a gamble against prices you haven't seen yet.

One practical note: Lowe's stores are closed on Thanksgiving Day, November 26. If your plan involves walking the aisles that morning, it's online only.

## How does the member early-access window work?

Members of MyLowe's Rewards and MyLowe's Pro Rewards can shop Black Friday Doorbusters online a day early, on **November 26**, before they open to everyone on November 27. Early access typically decides which Doorbusters you can actually buy — these are limited-time, limited-stock offers on select appliances, power tools, pre-lit trees, flooring, cabinets, ladders and paint.

The membership is free to join, and Lowe's said in March 2026 that it had more than 30 million members. Beyond early Doorbuster access, the holiday perks include:

- Free same-day delivery on qualifying orders over $25 (order by around 2 PM; size, weight and stock restrictions apply)

- Free member gifts and MyLowe's Money earning opportunities throughout the season
- Points on everything: 1x per $1 at the Bronze level, 1.25x at Silver ($500–$1,999 annual spend), 1.5x at Gold ($2,000+)

## What's actually worth waiting for?

Lowe's covers nearly every category in its Black Friday ad — the company posted more than $86 billion in fiscal 2025 sales across 1,750+ stores — but "on sale" and "at its lowest price of the season" are different things. Based on what Lowe's has confirmed about each phase, here's a reasonable split:

**Worth waiting for Thanksgiving week or Doorbusters:**

- Large appliances — they appear in both the Thanksgiving-week sale and the Doorbuster list
- Power tools and combo kits — DEWALT, Kobalt and CRAFTSMAN are all named in the Doorbuster lineup
- Outdoor power equipment
- Pre-lit artificial trees and Holiday Living décor — the New Kensington and Real Feel collections, plus a 5-foot poseable Elf on the Shelf if that's your thing

**Not worth waiting for:**

- A refrigerator that's already dead, a water heater that's leaking — urgent replacements don't get to wait three weeks
- Lumber and everyday project consumables, which generally sit outside the headline promotions
- Anything already discounted on October 29 at a price you're happy with — holding out for Thanksgiving week means betting on stock that may not survive

There's a third category people get wrong: gift cards. Lowe's sells them, they make fine presents, but they're essentially never on sale, and as you'll see below they're excluded from cash back at virtually every portal.

## The fine print that voids your cash back

This is the part deal roundups leave out, and it's where people lose the most money. These terms come from the portals' own published conditions for Lowe's:

- **Gift cards never earn cash back.** Buying a Lowe's gift card earns nothing at any portal, and paying with a gift card is excluded at several too.
- **Lumber, installation services and generators are excluded** at most portals. If your Black Friday project involves decking boards or a backup generator, assume zero cash back unless the portal's terms say otherwise.
- **Start with an empty cart.** TopCashback's terms require you to begin the purchase with nothing already in your Lowe's cart, or the tracking can fail.
- **Cash back is calculated on item price only** — before sales tax, shipping and fees. A 1% rate on a $1,000 appliance means about $10, not a percentage of your full receipt.
- **Don't use coupon codes from other sites.** Applying a code that isn't displayed on the portal can invalidate the cash back entirely. Stick to codes shown on the portal's own page.
- **Missing cash back claims have a deadline — usually 60 days from the order.** If the transaction doesn't show up in your account, file a claim promptly; after the window closes, the money's gone.
- **Returns kill the cash back.** If you return part of your order, the corresponding cash back comes back out.

And two TopCashback-specific gotchas that are easy to trip over during Black Friday rush:

- **Purchases made inside the Lowe's mobile app do not track for cash back through TopCashback.** Do the whole transaction in a desktop or mobile *browser* after clicking through from the portal.
- **Safari can break the tracking.** TopCashback's own page warns that Safari may not track properly — use Chrome or another browser for the click-through and checkout.

## Quick answers to common questions

**Is it better to shop Lowe's Black Friday early or wait?**
For appliances, tool kits and outdoor power equipment, Thanksgiving week is where Lowe's says its biggest savings land, and Doorbusters open November 26–27. For décor you need soon, urgent replacements, or something already discounted to a price you like on October 29, waiting mostly risks stock.

**Do I need a membership to get the deals?**
The October 29 deals are open to everyone. The November 26 early Doorbuster access and free same-day delivery require a free MyLowe's Rewards (or Pro Rewards) account, so sign up and log in before shopping.

**Does cash back stack with Lowe's coupon codes?**
Generally yes, as long as the code is displayed on the portal's own page. A code copied from a random coupon site can void the cash back.

**How long until cash back is payable?**
Cash back first appears as "In Progress," then becomes payable after Lowe's confirms the order — and portals typically allow returns windows to close first. Budget several weeks to a couple of months between purchase and withdrawal.

**What if my cash back doesn't show up?**
File a missing cash back claim with the portal within 60 days of the order. Have your order confirmation handy.
